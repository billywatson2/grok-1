A model's Elo only moves when someone votes, and it moves more against strong opponents. Ties count as half a win; “both bad” votes are recorded in the history but deliberately excluded from the ratings. With only a handful of votes the intervals stay wide — that is the honest state of a fresh arena, not a bug. Ratings are not comparable to any public leaderboard: they depend on which models are in this pool.
